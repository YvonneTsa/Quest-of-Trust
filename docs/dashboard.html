The Cost of Fraud · synthetic payments decision analysis

Evidence boundary: every event, fraud label, and dollar amount is synthetic. Results describe the simulator's assumptions, not a real processor, savings forecast, or production model.

Does the comparison repeat across seeds?

Each run changes the generated customers, payment events, endpoints, and attack campaigns; every holdout includes 350 planted fraud events. Thresholds are selected on earlier dates, then compared on later dates. The same fictional economics and review capacity apply to both strategies.

Ranges show the smallest and largest results among the simulated seeds. They are not statistical confidence intervals and do not establish real-world performance.
